Eco Tips

Reduce Vehicle Emissions

Combine errands into one trip to reduce cold starts, which produce more pollution. Consider carpooling or using public transport.

Improve Indoor Air

Use natural cleaning products, open windows when outdoor air is clean, and consider indoor plants that purify air like spider plants.

Energy Efficiency

Switch to LED bulbs and unplug devices when not in use. This reduces power plant emissions that contribute to air pollution.
